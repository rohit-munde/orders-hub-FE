Delete your OrdersHub account

OrdersHub by IndieDev

Delete from the app (fastest)

  1. Open OrdersHub and tap your profile picture at the top of the home screen.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Delete without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my OrdersHub account". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your OrdersHub account: name, email address, profile picture link and Google account ID.
  • Every imported order, with its items, amounts and statuses.
  • All connected Gmail accounts, their encrypted Google tokens and the record of which emails were imported.

OrdersHub's access to your Gmail is also revoked with Google.

What is kept

  • Merchant reference data (merchant names, websites and logos). It is shared by all users and contains nothing about you.
  • Copies in our hosting provider's backups, until those backups expire.
You can also remove OrdersHub's Gmail access at any time, without deleting your account, at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
