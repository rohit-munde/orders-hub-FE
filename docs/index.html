OrdersHub

Every order from every inbox, organized.

OrdersHub is an Android app that finds the order and shipping confirmation emails in your Gmail and turns them into one tidy list: what you ordered, from where, how much it cost, where it is now, and the delivery OTP when the courier needs one.

  • Read-only Gmail access, limited to order-related emails.
  • Email text is processed, not stored.
  • No ads, no data selling, no AI training on your data.
  • Disconnect Gmail or delete your account any time from inside the app.
